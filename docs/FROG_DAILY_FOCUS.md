# Eat the Frog daily focus exemption

Eat the Frog releases its block after **more than 30 minutes of focus logged on the current local calendar day**. Exactly 30 minutes does not trigger the exemption.

- Android counts explicit in-app focus logs and completed TickTick focus sessions. Task completions, notifications, leisure credits, and earlier days do not count.
- Chrome counts synced focus sessions, manual focus records, and completed local focus timers. A local timer and its uploaded copy count once. Synced evidence belongs to the signed-in account.
- The exemption releases an already active Frog block and prevents a new one for that day. The next day is evaluated using that day's focus, independently of the configured Frog wake hour.
- The saved Eat the Frog preference, selected task, completion tick, and tracked task progress are preserved. The exemption does not complete the task.
- Ordinary boundaries and permanent blocks continue to apply.

Desktop Eat the Frog remains disabled by its existing feature switch. This change concerns FocusLock's focus policy and does not modify the shared Void launcher or standalone Void.
