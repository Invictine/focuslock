03 — The loop

Work. Earn.
Spend. Lock.

Four verbs, one rule. Everything else in FocusLock — limits, schedules, commitments — exists to keep this loop honest.

01● focus

Work

Run a pomodoro or stopwatch in TickTick. Completed sessions sync in automatically — no logging, no honour system.

02● credit

Earn

Focus converts to leisure at your rate, tracked to the second. Unspent balance rolls into tomorrow.

03● leisure

Spend

Only the apps and sites you pick draw from it. Your maps, messages and music stay untouched.

04● boundary

Lock

At zero, everything you chose locks at once — phone, desktop and browser — until you've earned more.

The feed was designed to have no bottom. So we gave it a price.

04 — Boundaries

Choose how hard
it holds.

Some days a gentle budget is enough. Some weeks you need a wall you can't talk your way past. Climb only as far as you need.

  1. 01

    Balancealways on

    Spend what you earned. When it's gone, chosen apps and sites lock until you focus again.

  2. 02

    Limitsper app · per site

    A daily cap on the worst offenders — even when you have balance to burn.

  3. 03

    Schedulesrecurring windows

    Work hours, bedtime, Sunday mornings. Windows can wrap past midnight.

  4. 04

    Frogmornings

    Eat the frog first. From wake time, distractions stay shut until your one priority task has its focus time.

  5. 05

    Strictup to 30 days

    A commitment that can only be extended, never shortened. Early release needs a trusted person to approve it by email.

  6. 06

    Nukebreak glass

    Everything, everywhere, locked now. Coming back takes a ten-minute breathing cooldown and a concrete plan.
