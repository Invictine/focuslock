Rings Lab · 10 variations

J / K or ↑/↓ to step through

Every variant tracks the same two dashboard goals — focus minutes vs 120 and tasks vs 5 — so you're comparing presentation, not data. Drag the sliders.

01 · Baseline

Classic Twin

The current Android dashboard hero, re-ported to SVG: fat rounded pink/cyan strokes, tabular center % and "Focus", blue task badge pinned at the bottom edge.

02 · Direction

Neon Halo

Same twin geometry, cyberpunk register: glow filters, thin light strokes, 24 progress ticks in a halo. Reads "locked in", pairs with the Nuke overlay vibe.

03 · Direction

Segmented Blocks

Each ring built from 36 discrete segments (5-minute blocks, task ticks). Quantized lighting feels mechanical and game-y — progress earns "blocks".

04 · Direction

Gradient Sweep

Two 270° speedometer gauges stacked vertically, hue rotating red→gold and ice→mint along the arc. Friendly for "minutes remaining" reads.

05 · Direction

Paper & Ink

Cream ground, hairline strokes, serif numerals. Progress reads through overprint color, not weight. For a calm, editorial settings/summary page.

06 · Direction

Move · Focus · Guard

Apple-Watch triple play: Move (focus), Exercise (tasks), plus a new third ring — Guard, the share of the day you stayed inside your screen-time budget (demoed at 70%).

07 · Direction

Instrument Half-Gauges

Two 180° gauges stacked like a dashboard cluster. Zero-height friendly, reads at a glance — good if the hero has to shrink into a compact row.

08 · Direction

Orbit Comet

Progress lives in one comet-dot travelling a thin orbit, dragging a fading tail. The center is freed for streak counts. Tasks stays as a hairline inner loop.

09 · Direction

Beat Your Week

Rings get pressure: a 7-day bar column beside them colors each past day by how close you got to goal, with today highlighted. "Ring as personal best".

10 · Direction

Bento Mini-Chips

The ring demoted to seasoning: each goal becomes a bento card with a micro-ring in the corner. Best landing when the dashboard grows more stats than two.
